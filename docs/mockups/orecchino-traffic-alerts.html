Orecchino · ADS-B traffic alerts · design

Manned aircraft near the drones you hear

The receivers know where the drones are; the ADS-B feed knows where the aircraft are. Put together, they can say the thing that matters most: a manned aircraft is close to a drone. Every surface uses the same rules and the same words, and every one shows how old the data is.

T5 · e-paper

T5 table · the plot panel becomes a traffic card while a warning lasts

A traffic card, and the flash as the alarm

E-paper cannot blink, so a new warning forces a full refresh: the panel's black flash is the one motion the board can make, and it is used on purpose. The front light pulses three times with it, so the alert can be noticed across a room even in daylight.

  • The header leads with the traffic words; the row carries them too.
  • The card speaks relative to the drone: 1.1 km NE of D9A03, 90 m above it.
  • Data age is always in the card's top corner.
  • The two honest sentences stay on the card, where the decision is made.
T5 side view · the separation bracket and the aircraft's time ghost

Separation you can read at a glance

The side view already answers "how high". With traffic it answers the question that matters: how far apart vertically. A bracket joins the drone to the aircraft with the separation in metres and feet, and the aircraft trails a time ghost: a dot every 15 seconds along its next minute, which e-paper can show without animating anything.

  • Aircraft are outlined diamonds; drones are filled. They cannot be confused, in grey or in a hurry.
  • The right panel lists traffic pairs, most urgent first, instead of the ceiling list while an alert lasts.

Phone · iOS and Android

Lock screen · Dynamic Island and a time-sensitive notification

Clock position, the way pilots say it

The phone has what the boards lack: a compass. So the Dynamic Island (and Android's ongoing notification) gives traffic as a clock position relative to where the phone points, "2 o'clock", which is how traffic is called in a cockpit, and needs no map to understand.

  • Warnings are time-sensitive on iOS and a high-importance channel on Android; cautions are not.
  • One notification per drone-aircraft pair every five minutes; the Live Activity stays up while the alert does.
  • Actions: Show (opens the pair on the radar) and Mute 10 min.
Live radar · heading-up, with the separation bridge

A separation bridge

On the heading-up radar, a translucent bridge joins the drone to the aircraft it is close to, labelled with horizontal and vertical separation. As they converge the bridge shortens and thickens, so the danger reads as a shape before any text is read. The aircraft trails the same time ghost as on the T5.

  • A distinct haptic (three short pulses) for traffic, different from drone alerts.
  • An optional spoken callout, off by default: "Traffic, two o'clock, one point one kilometres, two thousand six hundred feet, descending."
  • Find mode can point its big arrow at the aircraft instead of a drone.

Mac app

Mac app · Traffic layer, alert list and the menu bar indicator

Traffic from any app

The Mac fetches the feed itself and draws a Traffic layer beside TFRs: aircraft rotated to their track with a dashed minute of projection, and the same separation bridge as the phone. Alerts sit above drone alerts in the sidebar, and the drone's card gains a "Nearest traffic" row.

  • A menu bar indicator: an airplane glyph with the count of aircraft within 3 km, red while a warning is active, so traffic is visible without switching to the app.
  • The Mac also passes the aircraft to the plugged-in receiver, so a T-Embed or SenseCAP without Wi-Fi alerts too.

What no surface ever says

Not every aircraft broadcasts ADS-B, and the feed has gaps and delay. So no screen says "clear", "no traffic" or "safe", and none says "collision" or "conflict": these are reported positions, not predictions. The most any screen says is no ADS-B traffic reported within 3 km, always with the age of the data. When the data is more than 30 seconds old, new alerts stop and every surface says TRAFFIC DATA STALE.
